## The art direction

**Still-life photography of bronze, bookbinding cloth and blank paper on a
seamless ivory ground, lit by one soft window light raking through a slatted
blind.** Three materials only — matte cream ceramic, dark forest-green
cloth and wax, and *dulled, patinated* bronze — plus pale mint paper cards.
Same light, same ground, same lens in all four.

Bail and surety are enforced with objects, not screens. A beam balance, a
wire-sealed box, a wax seal and a weight *are* the instruments of this product,
so a still life says "bond" with no blockchain imagery at all. And because the
page is cream paper set in Garamond, photographing real objects on that exact
cream under one shuttered window light makes the images read as plates in a
printed journal rather than art dropped on a website.

Two deliberate rules:

- **No clay red in any image.** `#C4553D` means "bond kept" in the interface.
  If red shows up in photographs, the one place it carries meaning loses it.
- **The slat shadows escalate** — faint in images 1–3, the dominant graphic
  element in image 4, where they echo the 90-day clock the page draws in HTML.

**Run all four in one ChatGPT conversation, in order.** For images 2–4 add one
extra line: *"same materials, same light, same ivory ground and same lens as
the previous image in this set."* That single sentence is the strongest
consistency lever these tools have.

ChatGPT gives square, **3:2 landscape (1536×1024)** or **2:3 portrait
(1024×1536)**. Ask for those and let the page crop.

## 1. Hero — "The level beam"

**Ask for 3:2 landscape (1536×1024).**

> A still-life photograph. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT ANYWHERE: no letters, no numbers, no words, no handwriting, no printing, no labels, no engraved or embossed characters, no logos, no watermarks — every paper surface in this image is completely blank.
>
> SUBJECT: a small, old, precise beam balance in dull bronze — a plain horizontal beam with two shallow pans hanging on fine linen cords — standing right of centre, its beam perfectly level and at rest. In the left pan, one thick sheaf of blank cream paper folded once, edges soft and slightly foxed, bound with a narrow dark forest-green cloth tape. In the right pan, a single squat cylindrical apothecary weight in dull bronze, sitting dead centre. On the ivory ground in front of the balance, one small blank pale-mint card lies flat with a second bronze weight resting on it, making a crisp contact shadow. The level beam is the whole point of the picture: it must read instantly as balanced, settled, resolved.
>
> COMPOSITION: the balance occupies the right two-thirds. The left third is empty ivory ground, crossed only by faint light bars, kept clear for a headline. Slightly elevated three-quarter view.
>
> MATERIALS AND WORLD: everything sits on a seamless sweep of matte ivory paper, colour #FFFFEB — a warm clotted-cream, clearly warmer than white — which fills the frame edge to edge, so there is no table edge, no horizon, no wall and no visible background edge. Only three materials appear: unglazed matte cream ceramic (#FFFFEB to #F2EEDB), dark forest-green bookbinding cloth and linen cord (#123A2C), and dulled unpolished bronze with a cool brown-grey patina — aged, faintly dusty, never bright yellow gold, never mirror-polished, never glittering. The only other colour is pale mint paper (#C9EFD5) on small cards. Ink-black accents (#1A1A1A) only in the deepest shadow.
>
> LIGHT: one soft window light from the upper left passing through a narrow slatted blind, laying fine, evenly spaced parallel bars of shadow across the ivory ground; long soft shadows fall to the lower right; deep soft shading, no hard specular hot spots, no flash.
>
> STYLE: shot as a still life for a well-set print magazine. 100mm macro lens, f/5.6, real depth of field, real contact shadows, real weight. Calm, editorial, confident. The ivory ground must run to all four edges at an even tone so the image blends into a cream page.
>
> EXCLUDE: no people, no hands, no faces, no robots, no brains, no screens, no LEDs, no glowing light sources, no circuit boards, no cables, no keyboards, no UI, no charts. No purple, blue, violet, cyan, neon, orange or red of any kind. No gradients, no glow, no bloom, no lens flare, no sparkles, no bokeh balls, no glassmorphism, no 3D-render or CGI plastic look. No vignette darkening the corners, no border, no frame, no drop shadow behind the whole image, no visible photo edge.

**Why it earns its place:** "Keep your word, keep your money" is an abstraction
until you see a beam at rest with paper on one side and money on the other. The
picture *is* the sentence.

**If it comes back wrong:** the beam tilts, or the bronze renders as bright
crypto-gold. Add — *"the beam is exactly horizontal, perfectly level, dead
even, both pans at the same height; the metal is dull aged bronze with a dusty
brown-grey patina, matte — not gold, not shiny, not yellow."*

## 3. The sealed verdict — "Wired shut"

**Ask for 2:3 portrait (1024×1536).**

> A still-life photograph. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT ANYWHERE: no letters, no numbers, no words, no handwriting, no printing, no labels, no engraved or embossed characters, no logos, no watermarks — the card and the seal face in this image are completely blank.
>
> SUBJECT: a heavy sealed strongbox: an unglazed cream ceramic body with dull bronze corners and a dull bronze lid, completely closed, with no window and no opening except one narrow horizontal slot across its front face. The lid is wired shut — a fine bronze wire passes through two lugs and is clamped by a small flattened bronze seal — and a dark forest-green linen cord is tied around the whole box in a cross, knotted once. One blank pale-mint card has emerged halfway out of the slot and hangs there, catching the light. To the right, on the ivory ground: a bronze hand seal — a turned handle with a plain blank round die face — lying on its side so the die catches the raking light, beside a shallow cream ceramic dish holding a dark forest-green ink pad. The box must read as an opaque, heavy, sealed physical container, like a bonded customs case or a sealed ballot box: it is shut and cannot be opened, what comes out of it is a single card, and the seal lying beside it is the human act that follows.
>
> COMPOSITION: portrait. The sealed box dominates the upper two-thirds, slightly left of centre; the hand seal and dish sit lower right with a clear stretch of empty ivory between them. Slightly elevated three-quarter view.
>
> MATERIALS AND WORLD: everything sits on a seamless sweep of matte ivory paper, colour #FFFFEB — a warm clotted-cream, clearly warmer than white — which fills the frame edge to edge, so there is no table edge, no horizon, no wall and no visible background edge. Only three materials appear: unglazed matte cream ceramic (#FFFFEB to #F2EEDB), dark forest-green bookbinding cloth, cord and ink (#123A2C), and dulled unpolished bronze with a cool brown-grey patina — aged, faintly dusty, never bright yellow gold, never mirror-polished, never glittering. > STYLE: shot as a still life for a well-set print magazine. 100mm macro lens, f/5.6, real depth of field, real contact shadows, real weight. Calm, editorial, confident. The ivory ground must run to all four edges at an even tone so the image blends into a cream page.
>
> EXCLUDE: no robots, no androids, no humanoid shapes, no faces, no eyes, no brains, no people, no hands. No computers, no servers, no screens, no displays, no monitors, no LEDs, no indicator lights, no buttons, no dials, no lenses, no keyholes, no circuit boards, no cables, no vents, no holograms, no UI. No purple, blue, violet, cyan, neon, orange or red of any kind. No gradients, no glow, no bloom, no light escaping from any seam, no lens flare, no sparkles, no glassmorphism, no 3D-render or CGI plastic look. No vignette darkening the corners, no border, no frame, no drop shadow behind the whole image, no visible photo edge.

pixel of AI iconography.

plain sealed ceramic-and-bronze container, nothing more."*

## 4. The clock — "Ninety bars"

**Ask for 3:2 landscape (1536×1024).**

> A still-life photograph. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT ANYWHERE: no letters, no numbers, no words, no handwriting, no printing, no labels, no engraved or embossed characters, no scale markings, no graduations, no logos, no watermarks — the paper and the seal face in this image are completely blank.
>
> SUBJECT: a tall slim hourglass with a dull bronze frame and plain clear glass, standing upright left of centre, filled with fine cream-white sand. Most of the sand has already fallen: the upper chamber is nearly empty and a thin stream is still running, untouched, with nothing and nobody near it. To its right, lying flat on the ivory ground: one folded sheet of blank cream paper closed with a thick disc of dark forest-green sealing wax, pressed and slightly spread at its rim, and beside it a bronze hand seal with a plain blank die, set down as if just used. The story is that time runs out by itself, and that a sealed sheet of paper is the only thing that buys more.
>
> COMPOSITION: landscape, built to be cropped into a wide band. The dominant graphic element is the light: the raking light through the slatted blind lays a long row of fine, evenly spaced parallel bars of shadow marching left to right across the entire ivory ground, and the hourglass and the sealed paper sit across those bars so the bars pass behind and beneath them. The bars must feel counted, regular and unbroken, like a measured scale of days, and should be noticeably stronger and more graphic here than in the other images of this set — they are as much the subject as the hourglass is. Slightly elevated three-quarter view.
>
> MATERIALS AND WORLD: everything sits on a seamless sweep of matte ivory paper, colour #FFFFEB — a warm clotted-cream, clearly warmer than white — which fills the frame edge to edge, so there is no table edge, no horizon, no wall and no visible background edge. Only three materials appear: unglazed matte cream ceramic and cream sand (#FFFFEB to #F2EEDB), dark forest-green sealing wax and bookbinding cloth (#123A2C), and dulled unpolished bronze with a cool brown-grey patina — aged, faintly dusty, never bright yellow gold, never mirror-polished, never glittering. A single small blank pale-mint slip (#C9EFD5) may peek from the folded sheet. Ink-black accents (#1A1A1A) only in the deepest shadow.
>
> LIGHT: one soft window light from the upper left through a narrow slatted blind; long soft shadows fall to the lower right; the overall image stays bright, light and airy, never moody or dim; no hard specular hot spots, no flash.
>
> STYLE: shot as a still life for a well-set print magazine. 100mm macro lens, f/5.6, real depth of field, real contact shadows, real weight. Calm, editorial, confident. The ivory ground must run to all four edges at an even tone so the image blends into a cream page.
>
> EXCLUDE: no clock face, no dial, no hands, no numerals, no tick marks, no engraved graduations on the glass or frame. No people, no hands, no faces, no robots, no brains, no screens, no LEDs, no glowing light sources, no circuit boards, no cables, no UI, no charts, no calendars. No purple, blue, violet, cyan, neon, orange or red of any kind. No gradients, no glow, no bloom, no lens flare, no sparkles, no bokeh balls, no glassmorphism, no 3D-render or CGI plastic look. No vignette darkening the corners, no border, no frame, no drop shadow behind the whole image, no visible photo edge.

**Why it earns its place:** the shadow bars are the same ninety ticks the live
clock component draws, so the photograph and the interface rhyme — the image
makes the page's own signature graphic feel like it came from somewhere real.

**If it comes back wrong:** it engraves numerals on the glass, or goes dark and
moody and loses the cream ground. Add — *"no clock face, no dial, no hands, no
numerals, no scale markings anywhere; the shadow bars stay soft warm grey on a
bright cream ground the colour of clotted cream, and the whole image stays
light and airy with no dark or moody tone."*

## Two things that will go wrong

**ChatGPT will add text.** It is the most common failure by a distance. When it
does, re-run with the no-text clause at the *start* as well as the end, phrased
as a property of the objects rather than a prohibition: *"every sheet, card,
slip, label, seal face and die face in this image is blank, unmarked and
unprinted."* Stating what the objects **are** beats forbidding what they must
not have.

**The cream may come back white or grey.** Add *"the background paper is a warm
ivory cream, the colour of clotted cream or old eggshell, clearly warmer and
yellower than white."* If a faint rectangle still shows against the page, the
slot already handles it — the CSS blends the image into the cream ground rather
than masking it.
